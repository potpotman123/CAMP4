# CMP-002: two independent research projects

This repository contains two separate projects. The independent forecast and valuation do not assume that historical sell-side forecasts were biased, and the historical study does not assume CAMP is undervalued.

## [Project 1 — CMP-002 worldwide revenue forecast and valuation](01_cmp002_forecast_and_valuation/)

Build an unbiased forecast for CMP-002 in SYNGAP1-related disorder and a defensible valuation for management, investors and banks.

1. Understand the current sell-side models and their assumptions.
2. Build a skeleton reverse DCF with appropriate preclinical development and operating costs, to infer the revenue expectations consistent with market value.
3. Independently build a top-down epidemiology-driven worldwide sales forecast.
4. Compare the results and explain any gap: patients, diagnosis, eligibility, uptake, pricing, timing, development risk, competition or valuation assumptions.

**Available:** 15 original CAMP sell-side PDFs (eight with extracted text; seven additional LSEG reports saved November 17), an inherited Street-reference dataset, and the extensive KPI/PoS/post-LOE audit research prompt. **Not yet completed:** the independent forecast, reverse DCF and matched KPI audit. See the [Project 1 guide](01_cmp002_forecast_and_valuation/README.md).

## [Project 2 — Historical sell-side forecasts for first-entry rare DMTs](02_first_entry_dmt_forecast_study/)

Investigate whether preapproval sell-side forecasts systematically underestimated sales for first-entry rare-disease DMTs, why errors occurred, and which cases are relevant to CMP-002.

**Available:** historical research reports, original sources, the approval-screen universe, 99 extended candidates, 20 analogue dossiers and reproducible checks. **Current conclusion:** case-specific upside and downside evidence, not a demonstrated universal underforecasting effect. See the [Project 2 guide](02_first_entry_dmt_forecast_study/README.md).

## How the projects connect

Project 1 may reuse Project 2's comparator evidence, with KPI-specific inclusion criteria. The approval-selected historical universe is not a valid denominator for probability of approval: Project 1 needs a cohort including failed and unresolved development programs.

CAMP sell-side originals live only in Project 1. Historical analyst estimates, the supplied prior analysis and approval-source data live in Project 2. Mixed historical evidence packages remain preserved in Project 2, with an explicitly inherited Street snapshot in Project 1. Cross-references avoid duplicating original PDFs.

The repository remains public under the user's explicit authorization, including supplied sell-side context. The [reorganization map](repository_reorganization_2026-11-16.json) records where every preexisting project file moved. Historical protocols and archived authoring scripts retain their original wording; their old paths can be resolved through that map.
