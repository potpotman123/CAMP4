DECISION
Evaluate materially improves discovery and disposition evidence, but does not supply a clean, automatically usable inception cohort. The qualified comparison panel expands from310 to317 molecule clusters, genetic CNS81 to88, and genetic medicines in genetic CNS48 to55. The four closest restoration ASOs remain unchanged. The updated ten-year cumulative approval estimates are21.6%,20.3%,20.9%, respectively; these are NOT lifetime or preclinical probabilities. Needham's25% remains an uncalibrated assumption, neither validated nor decisively rejected.

This is a completed bounded reconciliation of the existing310 and a targeted high-relevance expansion, NOT a completed historical census of every Evaluate rare-disease program. The remaining discovery queue is explicitly retained. Do not describe unmatched/discovery records as failures.

WHERE EXPANSION STOPS / REMAINING COVERAGE
The independent export comparison produces5,329 unmatched disease-linked vendor Product IDs, NOT5,329 eligible missed drugs. A reproducible high-relevance screen identifies40 genetic-medicine candidates with apparent clinical-phase history. Dispositions:8 reviewed program additions (7main clusters);7 post2024/not-yet-dosed watchlist;4 clinical only in other indications;3 prior entry-uncertain sensitivities;2 zero-enrollment pre-dose failures;1 possible existing construct alias; EGT101 awaiting firmer first-dose evidence;14 not yet qualified. All retained explicitly, none classified as failures simply because missing.

Newer candidates such as MVX220, RC001, SGT212 and ION337 are preserved in the watchlist. They are outside the original2010–2024 entry window, not deemed irrelevant. A separate current-era inception cohort should include them prospectively once actual exposure is confirmed. Changing the entry window only for newly discovered drugs would make the comparison inconsistent.

Completing a historical census across thousands of records would require substantially more collection: exact indication/construct linkage, first exposure, original versus later indication, US pathway and primary event histories. The standard exports do not supply those facts automatically. This bounded pass improves the existing analysis but does not establish coverage completeness or eliminate paper-trail selection. Do not claim otherwise.

VALUATION IMPLICATION
The more relevant clinical genetic-CNS tier shifts modestly downward, while two failed-to-active corrections strengthen the surviving pipeline. Neither effect calibrates eventual pre-dose CMP002 PoS.25% is still a sensitivity anchor; a new platform/target, pre-human-entry attrition and CNS translation require explicit judgment. Do not average the nested rates as independent estimates or substitute a ten-year probability for lifetime approval.
