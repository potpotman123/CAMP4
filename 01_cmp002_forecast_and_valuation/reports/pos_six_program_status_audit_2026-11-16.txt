SIX UNAPPROVED MEMBERS OF THE TEN-YEAR GENETIC-CNS GENETIC-MEDICINE SET
16 November 2026; reviewed without agents.

These are not six equivalent clinical failures. Replaces generic unknown labels with clinical result and development-disposition evidence. No approval event or entry date changes;3/10 ten-year and4/10 approved-by-cutoff arithmetic unchanged.

ISIS333611 — stopped_superseded
Initial trial focused on safety; termination was replacement by a more potent candidate, not a demonstrated pivotal efficacy failure.
Ionis developer-authored account explicitly says development was terminated in favor of an improved molecule; tofersen is distinct.
Remaining uncertainty: Exact decision date not established.
Evidence: First-party historical account by Ionis drug developers, published in a review; not an unrelated secondary inference.
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8682074/

AAVrh10-ARSA — clinical_efficacy_not_demonstrated_program_fate_unknown
Investigators report no clinical effect despite persistent CSF ARSA restoration, including in presymptomatic patients.
Negative clinical evidence established; no definitive current continuation or formal program-wide termination recovered.
Remaining uncertainty: Formal disposition and stop date remain unresolved. New vectors for MLD are not evidence this original product continues.
Evidence: Original research paper from investigators explicitly describing their prior clinical trial experience.
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8175802/

AAVrh10-CLN2 / LX1004 — sponsor_exit_rights_returned_successor_unknown
Early signal of slower progression; distribution limitations prompted consideration of a different delivery route and additional preclinical work.
Lexeo sought partners in2024; its September2026 quarter10Q states license terminated and CLN2 IND returned to Cornell inOctober2026.
Remaining uncertainty: Cornell continuation/relicensing not established. Sponsor exit is not proof of permanent molecule failure.
Evidence: Sponsor community letter and latest SEC filing.
https://bdsrafoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Lexeo_Letter-to-community_Feb2024.pdf
https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1907108/000119312526462020/lxeo-20260930.htm

Tominersen — stopped_efficacy
GENERATION HD2 met safety/biomarker objectives but showed no meaningful clinical efficacy benefit against placebo.
Roche explicitly discontinued clinical development July9,2026. This is the later HD2 decision, not simply the2021HD1 trial halt.
Remaining uncertainty: No current continuation established; exact disclosed stop date known.
Evidence: Dated sponsor letter.
https://ehdn.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Roche-GENERATION-HD2-_-POINT-HD-global-patient-community-letter-July2026.pdf

SAF301 — superseded_by_distinct_asset
Early study met its safety objective; successor engineering changed the promoter and removed SUMF1 to improve the construct.
Development shifted to distinct LYS-SAF302. No standalone ongoing SAF301 development recovered.
Remaining uncertainty: Exact formal SAF301 retirement date not established; this is not a verified pivotal clinical failure.
Evidence: Issuer annual registration document and developer presentation.
https://echanges.dila.gouv.fr/OPENDATA/AMF/190/8888/01/FC190380000_20210412.pdf
https://bettersciencebetterhealth.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/1-Michael-Hocquemiller-Advanced-Therapies-Opportunities-and-Challenges_-LYSOGENE-presentation.pdf

rAAV2/5-hNAGLU / AMT-110 — deprioritized_current_fate_unknown
Five-and-a-half-year followup found no confirmed cognitive benefit in three older children; youngest child had a more favorable course in an uncontrolled four-patient study.
uniQure explicitly deprioritized program November14,2016 and discussed discontinuation or transfer. Later patient followup publication does not establish renewed drug development.
Remaining uncertainty: Final transfer/termination and any current active successor not established.
Evidence: Sponsor filing and original long-term clinical report.
https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1590560/000110465916158478/a16-21899_1ex99d2.htm
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34405688/

For molecule-level approval PoS, replacement by a distinct molecule is original-molecule attrition; it does not show the target or platform failed. Formal discontinuation is not required to establish no approval within a past horizon, but unknown development fate must not become a confirmed permanent failure. The first-party ISIS account and latest Lexeo filing materially improve previously under-resolved records.
