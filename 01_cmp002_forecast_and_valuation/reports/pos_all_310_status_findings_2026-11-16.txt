DEFINITION AND SCOPE
The unit is the existing asset/combination cluster: 316 qualifying drug-indication records collapse to 310 clusters. The denominator is preserved, including failures and unknowns. These are not 310 perfectly independent molecular experiments; shared components, vectors, companies and targets create dependence. The 43 unresolved identity/entry records OUTSIDE the qualified panel are a different missingness problem and have not silently entered or left these 310.

Approval retains the prior endpoint: FDA approval in an included qualifying indication. It is historical ever-approval, not necessarily continuing marketing availability. Foreign approval and later-indication activity do not silently become original-indication FDA successes. This is therefore molecule-identity-specific, indication-scoped classification, not success anywhere in any disease. For merged clusters, an included indication approval wins; otherwise an active included indication wins; failure requires all included member rows to be failed; remaining combinations are unresolved. Individual member histories remain available.

Failure means the original candidate/program was stopped, abandoned, or superseded by a genuinely distinct candidate, for scientific, safety, strategic, regulatory or financial reasons. A hypothetical revival does not erase an observed stop. A different molecule's later success does not rescue the original candidate. Conversely, an ownership transfer, name change, replacement trial, temporary clinical hold or completed trial alone does not establish candidate failure.

Active means positive evidence of continuing clinical/regulatory development in the original included indication, including explicitly temporary holds. Some observations are registry attestations, not proof of continuous investment or current recruitment. Evidence dates and the basis are retained. Long-term follow-up alone can be insufficient: ACTUS101 is unresolved for advancement despite an active label. Missing date fields in the inherited summary are not automatically evidence of inactivity; source dates may appear in the detailed family record.

WHAT CAN ENTER THE MODEL
These classifications can enter candidate-specific attrition scenarios and an explicit active/unknown sensitivity. They do not establish Needham's 25% as right or wrong. Approval divided by approved-plus-failed would discard 115 active and 40 unresolved candidates and would be particularly misleading for the newer cohort. The 42/310 observed approval fraction is also not eventual PoS. Active candidates need time-to-event treatment with credible last-observed dates; unknown histories cannot simply be assumed to be non-informative censoring. Precise stop dates remain unresolved for some failures, even where current status is clear.

Prior fixed-horizon calculations are preserved. No all-drug or all-preclinical base rate is claimed. This clinical-entry panel does not directly measure pre-first-dose attrition for CMP-002. Coverage of discontinued candidates never captured by the public discovery frame is a separate potential selection bias; reducing status missingness cannot resolve it.
