Bias assessment
Selecting a cohort by entry date rather than outcome avoids a simple completed-outcome/survivorship error. It does not make that cohort representative of contemporary CMP-002 development. The ten-year result has substantial era and modality mismatch. Modern platforms, target knowledge, manufacturing, endpoints, regulatory routes, financing and development speed can differ, with no automatic direction of bias. The three mature genetic-CNS genetic-medicine successes are nusinersen, onasemnogene and elivaldogene; two share SMA biology. This is not an independent validation of SYNGAP1/regRNA.

Date-documentation selection and the original48-condition discovery frame create additional potential bias. The27-case sensitivity suggests the ten-year date boundary alone does not greatly alter the broad result; it does not repair era mismatch, missing programs or incomplete alias ascertainment.

Correct interpretation
28.4% is the observed ten-year approval fraction for an older selected panel, not a modern pre-dose lifetime PoS. The earlier findings should not foreground it as the principal CMP-002 benchmark. The public-source panel review is complete; a representative modern CMP-002 lifetime estimate is not established. The evidence neither validates nor statistically disproves Needham's25%.

Analyze entry-era groups at common observable horizons and matched modalities before considering any pooled estimate.
