CMP-002 PoS audit — decision memo
16 November 2026 | Project 1 | First public-source audit, not a complete global denominator

CONCLUSION
Needham's 25% is defensible as an optimistic judgmental approval assumption. The evidence does not establish it as conservative or empirically calibrated, but also does not prove it is too high. A generic 10% Phase-I benchmark is not an adequate rebuttal. CMP-002 is regulatory-cleared and awaiting first dosing, not a discovery-stage project.

If a single model input is needed now, 20% is a provisional judgment, with 10% downside, Needham's 25% explicitly retained, and 35% upside. These are scenarios, not estimated probabilities or a confidence interval. There is no statistical evidence here identifying a five-point difference between 20% and 25%; using a range without a preferred point is also defensible.

WHAT WAS BUILT
24 intrathecal CNS ASO assets / 28 drug-indication pairs with identified group-trial starts in 2010–2024. The underlying discovery query, 25 named/alias registry queries and seven targeted trial records are saved. The initial generic query returned 25 trials, not the whole clinical universe. Literature/pipeline discovery and sponsor termination disclosures filled gaps. Separate boundaries retain very recent entrants, individualized therapies, ocular/systemic programs and the older superseded SOD1 candidate.

At asset level: 3 approved; 7 stopped for efficacy; 1 stopped for disclosed strategy; 1 sponsor exit with asset fate unresolved; 12 still developing. These counts are not a lifetime approval rate. They are affected by incomplete coverage, different follow-up and correlated assets/targets/platforms.

CLOSEST PEERS
Zorevunersen / Dravet: closest DEE and deficient-protein restoration analogue. Early human signals and ongoing Phase III, not approval.
Apazunersen (GTX-102) / Angelman: close pediatric protein-restoration and functional-endpoint analogue. September 2026 pivotal primary/key-secondary failure; November filing confirms wind-down.
Rugonersen / Angelman: early human data; ongoing Phase III with OHB sponsor.
Obudanersen (ION582) / Angelman: ongoing Phase III at Ionis despite Biogen declining its option.
This four-program group has zero approvals, one stop and three ongoing programs. It is immature, not evidence of 0% PoS. Three molecules share Angelman/UBE3A biology, so four programs do not represent four independent biological tests.

EVIDENCE SUPPORTING 25%
Nusinersen validates protein-increasing intrathecal ASO treatment in a genetic disease, though SMA biology/endpoints differ. Zilganersen's September 2026 approval provides recent rare pediatric/adult CNS evidence, but through GFAP lowering. Tofersen demonstrates an accelerated approval pathway in genetic ALS, which cannot be assumed transferable to SYNGAP1. Ulefnersen's positive Phase III FUS-ALS results are supportive but are not an approval. CAMP has genetically causal biology, drug-specific mouse evidence and regulatory clearance. Independent adult mouse genetic rescue argues against a blanket assumption of irreversible disease after infancy.

EVIDENCE AGAINST CALLING 25% CONSERVATIVE
GTX-102 shows that early pediatric restoration signals need not survive a randomized pivotal trial. WVE-004 and BIIB105 show biomarker/target engagement without clinical benefit. BIIB078 and tominersen add genetic-CNS failures. CMP's regRNA transcriptional approach, human CNS distribution, safe dosage window, clinical reversibility and endpoint sensitivity remain unresolved. Successful ASOs do not directly validate the whole CMP-002 mechanism.

CLASSIFICATION CORRECTIONS
Do not count Biogen's ION582 option decision as asset failure. Do not count ION464/BIIB101 as permanently stopped solely from Biogen's exit: Ionis still lists it and HORIZON remains recruiting. Do not count BIIB080's missed Phase II primary endpoint as termination when development continues. Do not count a positive ulefnersen pivotal result as approval. Tofersen's approval is accelerated, not equivalent to traditional approval based on the same kind of endpoint CAMP would need.

VINTAGE AND STAGE
September GTX failure and zilganersen approval were already known at Needham's October 15 initiation; November GTX wind-down is later confirmation. Recent cases receive more qualitative emphasis; no arbitrary numerical recency weighting is fitted. Registry study start is generally an enrollment proxy, not the exact first dose. Ulefnersen had human individualized use before its group trial; NIO752's AD indication followed PSP exposure. Clinical-entry cohorts omit cleared programs that never dosed, so they cannot exactly estimate clearance-to-approval risk.

MODEL USE AND NEXT GATE
Use this work for explicit scenarios and causal risk assessment, not as a measured CMP-specific PoS. At fixed sales/timing, 20% versus 25% lowers probability-weighted revenue contribution by 20%, not necessarily total equity value by 20% because cash and development costs differ.
The next highest-value work is to verify the human target-engagement/endpoint bridge and reconstruct a fuller clearance-stage historical denominator, including never-dosed programs. Public registry records alone cannot do that. Paid collection or large historical reconstruction has not been initiated.
