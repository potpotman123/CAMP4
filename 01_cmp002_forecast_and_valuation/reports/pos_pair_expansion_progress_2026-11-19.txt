Expanded PoS analysis: extraction and pair-level preparation
19 November 2026 | Project 1 | INCOMPLETE: no new PoS estimates

Completed independent work
Parsed the two frozen Evaluate exports (24,845 source rows) into 32,187 candidate vendor-product/indication records spanning 9,515 vendor product IDs and 787 indication labels. Source file and CSV row references retained for every pair. This is the union of the existing orphan-any-designation and selected-disease exports, NOT an all-drugs census and NOT an all-rare-disease cohort.

22,042 pairs have a vendor clinical-stage/market signal. This is not proof of dosing in the 2010–2024 eligibility window. First clinical entry, indication-specific FDA approval, disease rarity and canonical molecule identity still require adjudication. These fields remain unknown; no current-status fraction has been presented as PoS.

866 pairs have both a current active-phase signal and an abandonment signal across source rows. This illustrates why a sponsor exit cannot automatically become global programme failure. All conflicting original statuses remain visible. All source indication-summary strings parsed without unmatched remainder; candidate IDs are unique. These are mechanical extraction checks, not independent clinical validation.

The broader CNS search needs indications outside the old genetic-CNS panel. The discovery inventory contains, for example, 28 clinical-stage-signal vendor pairs labelled neuromyelitis optica, 28 spinal cord injury, 27 frontotemporal dementia, and 8 progressive multifocal leukoencephalopathy. These are vendor discovery counts, not verified rare-disease eligibility, unique clinical molecules, or PoS denominators. They include repurposed agents and require prior-approval-at-entry flags.
