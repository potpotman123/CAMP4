What changed
The user successfully downloaded the unrestricted Evaluate CSV. The download blocker is resolved. The complete original is preserved losslessly as sources/evaluate_expansion_2026-11-19/all_status_all_indications.csv.gz, with hash and extraction scope in export_manifest.json. Public disclosure was explicitly authorized on 19 November.

All companies, products, indication classes and 43 WW status options were selected, including blank status, with no date restriction. The file contains 350,769 source rows, 116,650 vendor product IDs and 853 indication labels. Grouping company listings by vendor product ID plus indication label yields 199,342 candidate pairs. These are not yet canonical molecule-indication programmes: different brands, formulations, combinations and biosimilars can remain separate vendor products.

102,546 candidate pairs have a clinical-stage, regulatory or marketed-status signal; 96,796 do not. This is a discovery flag, not verification of clinical dosing or eligibility in the 2010-2024 entry window. All candidates remain in the ledger, including 50 pairs with entirely blank WW status.

Data needed for the PoS calculation
Only 5,014 / 102,546 clinical-signal candidates (4.9%) have any US IND filing date. An IND date is not first dosing. The export has no actual first-human/first-indication dosing field or dated phase-transition series. Neither phase-transition LOA nor a properly timed 5/10-year approval estimate can be calculated from this export alone.

10,535 clinical-signal candidates have a vendor indication-specific FDA date. A blank approval date is not evidence of failure or independently verified nonapproval. Across all candidates, 1,861 have multiple indication approval dates and 1,594 carry both active and abandoned clinical signals across company records. These remain uncollapsed for adjudication. Some differences may reflect licensing history, formulation or application differences rather than database errors.

Initial 24-record linkage check
Six candidates per four status/date strata were selected by a fixed hash rank. Strata: vendor FDA date present; active clinical without an FDA date; abandoned clinical without an FDA date; other/disputed clinical. Oncology was not separately balanced, a disclosed departure from the proposed joint stratification. This sample measures linkage problems, not PoS or population missingness.

All 24 received an initial registry/identity screen. Twenty name/alias searches returned at least one ClinicalTrials.gov hit; four returned none. Seven searches exceeded the bounded 100-record retrieval. Search hits are not validated programme matches. Zero of the 24 had an IND date in this export. Full historical eligibility and disease-specific rarity adjudication remain incomplete; this is not a completed 24-programme outcomes audit.

Findings that affect a larger calculation:
- DSP-0390/glioma: vendor status is abandoned-phase-I, but NCT05023551 lists an industry study as active-not-recruiting and NCT06636162 lists a Washington University study as recruiting. Preserve the conflict. Neither an outdated registry status nor a sponsor exit alone proves global failure.
- YHP2406/undisclosed: its two exact-code registry matches are phase-I bioequivalence studies from 2025. These cannot establish novel-drug inception in 2010-2024.
- Rilzabrutinib/ITP: FDA approval in 2025 is independently supported. The retrieved ITP study starts in phase II in 2018; an earlier pemphigus study must not supply ITP's entry date. Its renal programme is a separate sample requiring its own subtype and arm-entry history.
- Gynoxin: generic fenticonazole hits concern different fixed-combination products. NAL8801/galantamine patch, Vogelxo/testosterone and Enwylma/denosumab likewise need formulation or biosimilar checks before assigning originator histories.
- Four sampled records have vendor approval dates before 2010. New studies of old products must not reset clinical entry. Conversely, a 2014 approval does not establish first clinical entry after 2010.

Every sample member, query, raw registry response, initial assessment and unresolved issue is retained in data/pos_all_drugs_expansion and sources/evaluate_expansion_2026-11-19. Three dated trial leads (RG7386, LR19123, HM97662) are useful starting points, not fully corroborated first-worldwide-entry dates. No unmatched candidate was classified as failed.

Decision and next step
The inventory can enter discovery and coverage checks now. It cannot yet enter a PoS denominator. The next high-value input is indication-linked historical phase/first-entry information, followed by canonical molecule resolution and disease/subtype rarity mapping. FDA approval records help adjudicate successes but cannot supply an unbiased denominator.

An attempt to return to Evaluate's report-field catalogue encountered 'Multiple Login Warning / User not logged in'. The downloaded file remains safe. This did not establish whether the user's subscription has a separate historical-event export; do not conclude that Evaluate as a whole lacks these data. No account changes or security workaround were attempted.

Public ClinicalTrials.gov linkage works for discovery but requires role, formulation, indication and historical-version checks. Blindly assigning the earliest returned trial would produce the observed errors. A separate source scan identified commercial historical-series alternatives: AdisInsight Timelines documents monthly indication/phase/country snapshots since 2004. No new subscription or paid access was initiated. Do not scale to tens of thousands of manual histories without first measuring a suitable history source's coverage and discussing collection cost.

The intended nested funnel remains all captured therapeutic clinical programmes -> all rare diseases including oncology -> non-oncology rare -> CNS without a genetic-etiology restriction -> gene/RNA-directed medicines -> ASO -> intrathecal ASO -> deficient-protein restoration -> closest neurodevelopmental rationale. A separate all-CNS branch starts from all drugs. New cohort counts and PoS remain unavailable pending eligibility/history and disease mapping. Current-status fractions have not been substituted.

The old 319-molecule selected panel, genetic-CNS subsets and finite-horizon results remain frozen historical analyses. They are not relabelled as the all-rare or broader CNS funnel. This work provides no new empirical basis for increasing or decreasing Needham's 25% preclinical lifetime assumption.

Reproducibility

Counts reconcile to every source row. Parser/arithmetic checks are distinct from independent clinical validation.
