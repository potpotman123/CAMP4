Meaning of fields
- candidate_ids: every original screening bucket allocated to this row.
- trial_ids: panel registry records; additional_history_trial_ids and entry_evidence add relevant outside-panel history.
- entry_date/entry_basis: explicit first-human study start where verified; otherwise an explicitly named proxy, year or upper bound. Earliest trial start can predate a drug's addition to the protocol. It is not always first dosing.
- status: approved/stopped/ongoing/unknown in the named disease. jurisdiction and ever_FDA_approved_in_this_indication distinguish FDA from non-US outcomes. No later-indication approval credits.
- eligibility: strict first-human cohort inclusion/exclusion or pending. reference_cohort_eligibility permits a separately labeled finite reference inventory while retaining pending exact-entry problems. approval_probability_input_eligible stays false pending reconciliation/completeness.
- tier_flags: rare/geneticCNS/geneticmedicine/noveltarget/intrathecal/proteinrestoration; unknown allowed. None of the assigned systemic programs is a genetic CNS intrathecal comparator.
- only_mapped_trials_zero_enrollment_withdrawn is a property of retrieved records, not a global never-dosed assertion. Four rows have only zero-enrollment withdrawn mapped records; two are established medicines(eptacog alfa,deferoxamine), two are cell/transplant records. These do not become clinical failures.
