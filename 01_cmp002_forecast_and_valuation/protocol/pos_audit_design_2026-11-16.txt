PoS audit v1 — 16 November 2026

Question: Is Needham's 25% probability of first US approval for CMP-002 defensible at its regulatory-cleared, pre-first-dose stage? This is a Project 1 development-risk audit, not the Project 2 commercial-bias study.

Design: purpose-built, public-source reference universe of intrathecal ASOs targeting CNS disease with an identified group-trial start in 2010–2024. Novel-target matching and genetically defined pediatric protein restoration are separate subsets. Use 2025–2026 entrants and individualized/ocular/systemic approaches as named boundaries rather than cherry-picking their outcomes into the main tally. The pre-2025 boundary limits extreme immaturity but does NOT guarantee mature follow-up. Show entry vintages separately. No claim of blinded selection: some outcomes were already known, and criteria were documented during collection, not prospectively preregistered.

Discovery: one ClinicalTrials.gov query '(antisense OR oligonucleotide) AND (intrathecal OR intraventricular)' returned 25 trial rows, all retained with dispositions. That search misses ASOs whose registry text lacks the terms. Supplement with 25 named/alias intervention queries informed by historical neurological ASO literature and sponsor pipelines, then seven specific entry/pivotal trial records. Preserve all registry responses, including zero-result aliases. Search sponsor discontinuation disclosures, not only active pipelines or approvals. This is a representative reference set, NOT an exhaustive global clinical or preclinical denominator. Trials outside ClinicalTrials.gov, forgotten assets and undisclosed preclinical attrition remain coverage gaps.

Unit: one asset–indication pair; report unique assets separately because NIO752, VO659 and WVE-004 span diseases. Multiple trials are not separate programs. Explicitly flag correlated Wave compounds and shared Ionis chemistry, target families and sponsor infrastructure. Independent statistical observations cannot be assumed.

Starting stage: registry actual study-start date is an enrollment proxy, NOT automatically first dose. Preserve actual/estimated status and record specific dose dates where supported. Zorevunersen dosing began August 2020 versus June registry start. Ulefnersen had earlier individualized human exposure before its 2021 group trial; NIO752 had PSP exposure before AD. Neither is a clean no-prior-human-data landmark at the recorded group-trial date. Clinical entrants already survived preclinical/clearance risks; no additional generic discovery-stage haircut is justified for CTA-cleared CMP-002. Conversely, this design misses cleared programs that never dose, so no exact pre-first-dose approval probability is identified.

Outcomes at 2026-11-16: approved (traditional versus accelerated separately); stopped for efficacy; stopped for disclosed strategy; sponsor exit with asset fate unresolved; ongoing, including after partner exit or a failed endpoint; positive pivotal but not approved. A failed trial is not automatically a terminated asset. Keep evidence-date versus event-date explicit. Do not treat old reporting dates as verified continuous follow-up. These limitations preclude a fitted survival estimate in v1.

Needham date: assess 2026-10-15 separately from updated audit. GTX-102's September pivotal failure and zilganersen's September approval were already public; November GTX wind-down and later sponsor updates are subsequent evidence. CAMP UK clearance predates initiation; EU clearance is an October/November update. Do not describe today's ongoing Phase III peers as evidence available before those peers first dosed.

Rates: raw counts only for the primary conclusion. Optional complete-calendar-follow-up 5-/10-year counts are descriptive diagnostics, not lifetime PoS. The five-year boundary can fall just before an approval; do not infer zero chance of success. No Kaplan-Meier approval curve treating failures as independent censoring; no resolved-only proportion as an approval estimate; no binomial confidence interval implying independent/random sampling. Historical approval and discontinuation rates do not identify CMP-specific calibration with this dataset.

Decision standard: distinguish '25% is possible/defensible as an optimistic assumption' from '25% is empirically estimated' or '25% is conservative'. A provisional model input may be proposed only as explicit judgment, with sensitivity and no false statistical precision. No evidence-based exact replacement point estimate is claimed.

Sources: primary regulator, registry, sponsor disclosures and original trial reports determine core status; reviews are discovery leads except clearly labeled legacy boundary context. Preserve provenance, raw registry fields and competing interpretations. Needham's 25% is a financial assumption, not an observed clinical probability.

Stopping: public-source bounded audit can support a qualified decision now. Exact calibration requires broader historical pipeline/clearance reconstruction and full time-to-event history. Do not expand into paid databases, large OCR, broad scraping or complex fitting without explaining cost and obtaining authorization. No agents, paid access or outreach used.
